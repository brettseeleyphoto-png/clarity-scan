The solution

Not a course. Not a personality test.
Not a prompt pack.

The Clarity Scan is a guided discovery process — a structured conversation run through ChatGPT or Claude — that interviews you the way a documentary filmmaker would.

Not asking for your elevator pitch. Not asking for your mission statement. Asking for real examples. Specific clients. Actual frustrations. The opinions you'd say in a parking lot after a shoot but would never write in a caption.

By the end, you have a short profile — how you see the world, what you stand for, what makes you different, who naturally connects with your work, and how you actually sound when you stop trying to sound professional.

Then you use that profile to brief AI. And the output changes.

Before you start

Answers to the things people actually wonder.

I don't know what to say.
Good. The scan doesn't want polished answers. It wants real ones. You'll be asked about specific experiences, specific clients, specific frustrations. You'll have more to say than you think.
I don't think I'm that unique.
You're not being asked if you are unique. You're being asked to describe how you see things, what you notice, and what you care about. That information exists. You just haven't organized it yet.
I'm not good at talking about myself.
Most of the people who find this most useful say that. The questions aren't about you abstractly. They're about specific things that happened — specific clients, specific moments, specific opinions.
I don't know if my answers are right.
There are no right answers. There are vague answers and specific answers. The more specific, the better the output. The AI will push back if your answer is too general.
This feels weird.
It is a little weird. You're having a structured conversation about how you think and what you value. Most photographers have never done that. Weird is usually worth it.
I don't have time.
20 minutes. Or split it across a week. That's the ask.
What if AI gets it wrong?
The output is a starting point — not a final verdict. You'll recognize what's accurate, disagree with what isn't, and build from there. The profile is yours. Nothing is permanent.
Worth understanding
Why generic content underperforms
When AI has no real context, it fills in with averages — average language, average positioning, average tone. The output isn't wrong. It's optimized for nobody in particular. Giving AI specific, real context about how you think and who you're for changes what it can produce. This isn't a feature of AI. It's a feature of input quality.
On clarity and client decisions
Buying decisions — especially creative services — are heavily influenced by recognition. When a potential client reads your content and thinks "this person thinks the way I think," they're not responding to your skills. They're responding to clarity about who you are, who you're for, and what you believe. Generic content makes that recognition impossible.
Note: The principle that trust and familiarity influence purchasing behavior is broadly accepted in consumer psychology. Specific statistics should be verified against primary research before reliance.

FAQ

Straight answers.

Do I need a paid ChatGPT or Claude account?
The free versions of both work. A paid account may give you access to more capable models and improve output quality, but is not required.
Is this a course I have to complete?
No. There's no video content, no modules, no progress bar. You get a document — the master prompt — and a guide. You paste the prompt into AI and answer the questions in chat.
What if I've already tried using AI for content?
That's exactly who this is for. The scan isn't teaching you to use AI. It's building the context that makes AI output actually useful.
How is this different from other brand voice products?
Most brand voice tools start with your audience and work backward. This starts with how you see the world and works outward. The goal is not to craft a persona — it's to excavate the one you already have.
Can I use this more than once?
Yes. The scan is a starting point. As your work evolves, your answers change. Running it again six months from now will produce a more refined output.
What if I don't like the output?
The output reflects the input. If it feels off, the scan gives you a push-back mechanism — you can tell AI where it missed and ask it to recalibrate. Nothing is locked in.
